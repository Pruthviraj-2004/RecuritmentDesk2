# User guide

**1. Sign in.** The first administrator is created by `scripts\create-admin.ps1` (you choose the password).

**2. Connect Gmail** (*Connected Accounts → Connect Gmail*). Google's consent screen opens; approve read access (and send access if you want to email exports). Repeat for each account. Cards show status (Connected, Syncing, Reauthorization needed…), last sync, counts and the last error. *Settings* per account: display name, import window, extra Gmail search terms, auto-sync on/off. *Use as default sender* picks the account for outgoing exports. *Disconnect* revokes access at Google and deletes the stored credentials (imported data is kept).

**3. Sync.** *Sync now* (or *Sync all accounts*) starts a background job. *Full re-import…* re-reads a chosen window without creating duplicates. Watch progress in **Synchronization Jobs**: counters, attempts, errors, cancel and retry. If an account's authorization expires, sync pauses and a banner asks you to reauthorize.

**4. Only recruitment email is kept.** There is deliberately no mailbox/inbox screen. A sync asks Gmail only for mail that mentions hiring-related terms (requirement, hiring, job description, opening, vacancy, JD…), then discards anything clearly unrelated before it is stored (the job shows a "skipped_unrelated" counter). You can add your own Gmail search terms per account, or turn the filter off under *Settings → Gmail*. Mail you want to remove from RecruitmentDesk after the fact: `python -m app.cli purge-unrelated --yes` (deletes only RecruitmentDesk's copies, never Gmail). **Your own exports are never read back.** Mail carrying RecruitmentDesk's export marker (or the Message-ID of a delivery it sent), a digest you send from one of your connected accounts with a spreadsheet attached, and outgoing-only mail (Sent folder, not in Inbox) are skipped, so emailing yourself a report of 10 jobs does not create 10 new requirements. Mail from your own account *without* a spreadsheet (for example a forwarded job) is still processed, and the duplicate matcher attaches reformatted copies of an existing job instead of creating a new one. If duplicates were created before this rule existed: `python -m app.cli cleanup-own-exports` (dry run) then `--yes`. Extraction problems appear under **Synchronization Jobs → Processing issues** (with *Retry all*); a requirement's **Source emails** tab shows the subject, sender and an *Open in Gmail* link.

**5. Review requirements** (*Recruitment Requirements*). Search, filter (all the fields in *More filters*), sort and page on the server. Row menu: view, edit, approve, reject, archive/restore, source emails, version history, find similar, export. Select rows for bulk approve/reject/archive/restore/export. Records that need verification show warnings (e.g. a later email disagrees about the salary). Edits create a new version; nothing is overwritten silently.

**5b. Candidate Profiles** (*Candidate Profiles*). Your talent database. **Name, email and phone are required; every other field is optional.** Add people three ways: **Add candidate** (one form), **Import file** (CSV or Excel using the standard template), or a file with your own column names (common names like *Mobile*, *Key Skills*, *Full Name* are recognised). The import always starts with **Check file**, which shows what will be added, what already exists and which rows have problems, and saves nothing; then **Import**. Duplicates are found by email or phone in any format, and you choose to skip them or update the existing profile (name, email and phone are never changed by an update). Each profile has a status (new to hired/rejected), 1-5 rating, tags, skills, notice period, expected CTC, a notes timeline and a **pipeline** showing which requirements the candidate is in and how far they got (shortlisted, submitted, interview, offered, joined, rejected, withdrawn). **Matching jobs** on a profile and the **Candidates** tab on a requirement rank the best fits by required skills, experience and location, and say why. Search, filter, bulk-change status or tags, mark contacted, archive and export to Excel from the list.

**5d. AI Profile** (candidate page, *AI Profile* tab). On request, an AI agent turns a person's profile text into a structured summary: skills (with the top ones highlighted), experience timeline and **recent companies**, education and certifications, high-level projects, career highlights, and suggested screening questions. **LinkedIn does not allow automated reading of profiles, so RecruitmentDesk never fetches the LinkedIn link itself.** Open the profile while signed in, copy its text or use *More > Save to PDF*, then paste it or upload the PDF (a resume as PDF/DOCX/TXT works too) and click *Extract with AI*. The text is sent to your configured AI provider for that one request and is **not stored**; only the extracted result is kept (with a history of runs). Everything the AI returns is checked against your text: employers, skills, schools, links and contact details that are not actually in it are removed. Experience, average tenure, employment gaps and short roles are **calculated from the dates**, not guessed, and shown as neutral "worth verifying" prompts. *Apply to profile* lets you tick which parts to copy onto the candidate (name, email and phone are never changed), .

**Job matching uses both.** Matching (the candidate's *Matching jobs* tab and a requirement's *Matching candidates*) evaluates the union of the saved profile and the latest AI profile: skills from both (including skills used in roles and projects), the higher experience figure, locations from both, and role titles from the current role and work history. Nothing has to be applied first; matches show "Includes the AI profile" and which skills were found only there.

**5c. Demo candidates and templates.** For demonstrations, `python -m app.cli seed-demo-candidates --yes` creates 3-5 clearly marked dummy candidates (source *demo*, tag *demo*, e-mail @demo-candidates.example.org) for every active requirement, with strong, partial and weak fits, and puts some of them in pipelines. Re-running never duplicates them, and `python -m app.cli purge-demo-candidates --yes` removes only those demo profiles. Ready-to-fill import files are in the `templates` folder: `candidate-import-template.xlsx` / `.csv` (with two example rows and, for Excel, an Instructions sheet) and `candidate-import-template-blank.xlsx` / `.csv` (headers only).

**6. Duplicates.** Exact duplicates (same client + job ID, identical fingerprint) are attached automatically as extra sources. *Probable* duplicates appear in **Duplicate Review**: compare side by side, then **Merge** (choose which record survives; both records' sources and history are preserved; irreversible), **Keep both**, **Mark related**, or **Dismiss**. Same job title alone never creates a match. Different job IDs are never merged.

**7. Export.** *Export* from the list (selected rows, current filters, or all approved) or *Exports & Deliveries → New export*. Choose file name, worksheet, columns, sort and extras. The workbook has one row per unique requirement, a frozen formatted header, filters, wrapped descriptions and a summary sheet. Download from the export list (files expire after `EXPORT_RETENTION_DAYS`).

**8. Email an export.** *Compose email*: pick the sending account, attachment, recipients/CC, template, subject and message. *Review & send* shows a confirmation with sender, recipients and attachment; nothing is sent before you confirm. Statuses: **Accepted by Gmail** (accepted for sending; inbox delivery is not guaranteed), **Failed** (not sent, safe to retry), **Uncertain** (network failure; check your Gmail *Sent* folder; retry checks Sent first and asks you to acknowledge a possible duplicate).

**9. Analytics, Audit, Settings.** Analytics shows only metrics that have underlying data. Audit lists who did what. Settings: workspace defaults, Gmail, AI (status + health check), deduplication thresholds (affect future emails only), notifications, security (password, sessions, retention) and system health.
